My Trip to Udaipur

Udaipur is one of the most beautiful cities in Rajasthan. I visited City Palace, Lake Pichola, and enjoyed delicious local food. The sunset at Fateh Sagar Lake was unforgettable.

The city is known for its lakes, palaces, and rich cultural heritage. I would love to visit again.
