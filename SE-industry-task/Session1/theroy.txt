5. What is the difference between a developer and a tester? (2 lines)
Answer:- 
Developer: A developer writes, develops, and maintains software or applications based on project requirements.
Tester: A tester checks the software for bugs, verifies that it works correctly, and ensures it meets quality standards before release.
